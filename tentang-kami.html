Our Story
From a kitchen to three branches

Puan Kamariah & Encik Saifulizam at the grand opening of Maria's Bake & Brew

Maria's Bake & Brew began with the dream of two Muslim entrepreneurs from Muar — Puan Kamariah and Encik Saifulizam — who believed that food made with sincerity could bring people together.

Their journey started with Marialuvcakes, the first shop on Jalan Sulaiman in Muar — offering fresh bread, whole cakes, cookies, festive kuih, and a wide range of desserts. Each festive season, Marialuvcakes also rolls out a special collection of kuih raya.

That success gave birth to Miamor Coffee House — a private room dining concept for dine-in, walk-in, and online bookings for occasions like birthday celebrations and special events.

And now, Maria's Bake & Brew stands as the third branch — a petite cafe that brings together quality beverages, exclusive viral desserts, and a cozy atmosphere for everyone.
