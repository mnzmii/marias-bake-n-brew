How to find us

From Muar Town

Take Jalan Sulaiman towards Jalan Abdullah. The shop is in a white building with a green door — easy to spot.

Parking

Street parking is available along Jalan Abdullah. Public parking nearby can also be used.

Nearby Landmarks

Close to Muar's old town area. Look for a white colonial-style shophouse with a green balloon arch.
